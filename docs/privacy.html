Privacy Policy

Flexo League app (Android and iPhone: com.flexoleague.app). Краткая версия на русском ниже.

What we collect

  • Account: email, or sign-in with Google or Apple; nickname, age, sex.
  • Fitness and health: height, body weight, workouts and lift records, nutrition, water, sleep, Body Progress photos.
  • Content you post: posts, photos and videos in the feed, comments, likes, reports, clan, friends, duels.
  • Technical data: account ID, device model and app error reports, used to sync your data and fix crashes.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: save your progress in the cloud, calculate trophies and arenas, show leaderboards, the feed, clans and duels, and analyze food and activity with AI.

Who can see it

Other players can see your nickname, avatar, rank, trophies, lift records, weight class, clan and the content you post. Your body weight, height, nutrition, sleep and Body Progress photos are visible only to you.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in. Connections are encrypted.
  • Cloudflare R2: storage and delivery of photos and videos you post in the feed.
  • Google Gemini: AI analysis of food and activity text, and translations. Used only after you agree in the app. We send the text of your request and the profile values needed to estimate calories (sex, age, height, weight), without your name, email or photos.
  • Google or Apple, if you sign in with them.

There are no ads. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account and all your data, including posted photos and videos, in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. More details: how to delete your account.

Children

The app is for users aged 14 and older. If you are younger, please do not use the app.

Health

Flexo League is not a medical service and does not give medical advice. If you have an illness or injury, talk to a doctor before training.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date below.

Кратко на русском

Используем их только для работы приложения. Другие игроки видят никнейм, аватар, ранг, кубки, рекорды, весовую категорию, клан и посты; вес, питание, сон и фото прогресса видите только вы.
