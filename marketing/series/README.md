# Flexo League cartoon series

One recurring character in a 3D toon-shaded cartoon (skill `.claude/skills/inkbeat-shorts`, Remotion + three.js). He wears a hoodie/T-shirt with the
Flexo League logo (`marketing/assets/icon.png`). Every episode is a storytelling video of about 60 s (vertical, voice-over, captions) about his life
around the gym; the last 5-8 s lead the viewer to the app (CTA). Files:
- `bible.md`: the character (look, voice, personality), the world, tone and the story arc. The director owns it, the owner approves changes.
- `episodes/NN_title/`: `script.txt` (voice-over text), `plan.md` (the skill's plan), `status.md`.
- `marketing/cartoon/`: the Remotion project (copied from the skill's `templates/`), with `vo/` for voice files and `out/` for renders.
