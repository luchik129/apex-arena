# Flexo League content playbook (shared by all marketing agents)

## Product facts (only claim these)
Flexo League is a free gamified gym app (iOS + Android). Every workout earns trophies; 17 ranks from Basement to Legend;
7-day duels with friends; clans and weekly clan battles; lift tracking with PR charts; calories, sleep, water;
exercise technique guides; honest rating with anti-cheat (suspicious lifts are reviewed). No ads in the app.

## Audience
US gym-goers 16-30, mostly beginners to intermediate, who like games (Clash Royale, ranked modes, Duolingo streaks).
Language: casual US English, gym slang ok (PR, leg day, gains, gym bro), no Russian.

## Hard rules
- Show only real app screens from `marketing/assets/`. Never invent features, numbers of users, ratings, prizes or awards.
- No health or weight-loss promises, no medical claims, no body shaming, no competitor names or logos.
- No copyrighted music, characters, memes images or brand logos. Music comes from `engine/beat.py`.
- Text must be readable with sound off. Max ~5 words per line, max 3 lines per scene.
- Hook must land in the first second. Total length 7-12 s. Last scene is always `cta`.
- Nothing is published without the owner's explicit OK.

## Hook formulas that usually work on gym TikTok
POV ("POV: your gym is ranked now"), callout ("If you skip leg day, don't download this"), story ("He said I skip workouts... so I challenged him"),
rank flex ("Rate my rank"), relatable pain ("Me after 3 weeks of no progress"), challenge ("Can you reach Legend in 30 days?"),
contrarian ("Stop lifting for nobody"), question ("Which rank are you?").

## Posting
1 video a day, 6-9 pm US Eastern. Same video goes to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
Captions: 1-2 short sentences + 4-6 hashtags (always #gymtok, plus topic tags). YouTube title ends with #shorts.
